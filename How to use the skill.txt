Claude SaaS Animation Skill

1. Make a folder for your ad, for example "Spotify".
2. Put saas-animation.skill in that folder.
3. Add a high resolution logo and real screenshots of your app or product.
4. Give Claude access to the folder.
5. Ask for a numbered storyboard. Approve it or ask for changes before anything is animated.
6. Add your sound effects and music to the folder once you're happy with the visuals.

For 3D product ads, install Blender as well. It's free and you never have to open it:
https://www.blender.org/download/
